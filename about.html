About 2BS College Ball

What is 2BS College Ball?

2BS College Ball is a fictional college football universe built for fun, creativity, and community. It’s a custom‑crafted league with its own teams, owners, schedules, mascots, lore, and stats — all powered by a homemade scoreboard engine and a growing world of stories, rivalries, and personalities.

This project blends sports fandom, game‑design energy, and creative world‑building into one place. It’s not meant to replace real college football — it’s meant to celebrate the spirit of it.

A Living Fictional League

Every team, matchup, ranking, and storyline in 2BS College Ball exists inside this fictional ecosystem. Games are simulated. Standings are generated. Owners manage teams, build identities, and shape the narrative.

The league is designed to feel familiar — but everything inside it is original content created for entertainment and community engagement.

Not Affiliated With Any Real Organization

2BS College Ball is not affiliated with the NCAA, any real college or university, any athletic conference, or any real sports organization. Names, colors, and references are used in a parody, homage, and fictional context. Nothing here is official, commercial, or endorsed.

Why We Built This

Because sports are fun. Because world‑building is fun. Because making schedules, logos, mascots, and lore is fun. Because creating a shared universe with friends is fun.

2BS College Ball is a place to compete without pressure, build teams without limits, tell stories without rules, and enjoy football without the noise.

How the League Works

The league runs on a custom data engine that loads team and owner info, generates schedules, simulates games, tracks scores and standings, and builds a season narrative week by week.

Everything you see — from mini game cards to full tables — is generated from this engine.

Community & Participation

Owners participate by managing their teams, contributing lore, building mascots and identities, reacting to weekly results, and adding flavor to rivalries and matchups. This is a collaborative creative project. Everyone involved helps shape the world.

A Note on Imagery & Names

Some team names, colors, or mascots may resemble real‑world schools or sports brands. These similarities are non‑commercial, non‑official, and purely fictional.

All logos, mascots, and graphics used in 2BS College Ball are original creations, parody or homage, fan‑made interpretations, or non‑commercial assets. If any real organization ever requests a change, we’ll update the content immediately.

The Spirit of 2BS

At its core, 2BS College Ball is a creative project, a parody sports universe, a community game, a storytelling engine, and a place to have fun.
